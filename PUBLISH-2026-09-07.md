## Angle

Fresh vs. all 19 existing posts (verified: 0.30% 8-gram overlap at worst, one shared boilerplate definition sentence).

News hook: the FTC's proposed enforcement policy statement on **personalized pricing**, issued 2026-08-19 with a 30-day comment window. The Commission defines the practice as using consumer data to estimate an individual's willingness to pay, and its remedy is disclosure.

Core claim: that remedy assumes the merchant must *acquire* the data. On transparent agentic rails it acquires nothing — the agent's wallet address arrives with the payment and already publishes balance, spend rate, counterparty graph, realized prices, retry/comparison behavior and refill cadence. That is not an estimate of willingness to pay; it is close to a direct measurement. Four structural failures of the disclosure remedy are argued: no collection practice to disclose, no consumer in the loop at quote time, personalization upstream of the interaction, and the pricer need not be the merchant (resellable wallet-scoring APIs).

XMR402's counter: the 402 challenge is computed from resource cost alone, before any payer is visible; settlement lands on a single-use subaddress verified by Monero TX Proof (~200ms, 0-conf); statelessness means no accumulating payer record for a future pricing model — including the merchant's own.
